About A.C. Heating Services

A Family Business with Deep Roots in Guernsey

Established in 1984, A.C. Heating Services Ltd has been serving customers in Guernsey for more than four decades.

Originally operating in heating services, the company has evolved into an established electrical contracting business with expertise in commercial electrical installations, fire alarm systems, inspection, testing and maintenance.

Today, we continue to operate as a family-owned and family-run business from our base in St Martin.

We are proud to employ local people and maintain longstanding relationships with businesses, property managers and private customers across the island.

Our focus remains on delivering quality work, providing responsive service and developing lasting customer relationships.

Experienced, Local and Customer-Focused

We believe that successful electrical contracting is built on technical competence, professional workmanship and reliable customer service.

We take pride in working with customers to understand their needs, whether the requirement is a new installation, a fire alarm system, an electrical inspection or ongoing maintenance.

Our established local presence allows us to maintain close working relationships with the people and organisations we serve.

NICEIC Certified Since May 2013

A.C. Heating Services Ltd has maintained NICEIC certification since May 2013.

Our company is certified under the following schemes:

  • NICEIC Approved Contractor Scheme
  • NICEIC Domestic Installer Scheme
  • NICEIC Portable Appliance Testing (PAT)

Our longstanding certification reflects our commitment to recognised professional electrical standards.
