Commercial Electrical & Fire Alarm Maintenance

Dependable Maintenance Support for Guernsey Businesses

Managing commercial premises requires a reliable approach to electrical and fire alarm maintenance.

A.C. Heating Services works with Guernsey businesses to provide planned inspection, testing and maintenance services, together with reactive support when electrical problems arise.

Our approach focuses on developing ongoing relationships and understanding the needs of each customer’s premises.

Proactive Electrical Maintenance

Planned maintenance can help identify electrical issues before they develop into more significant problems.

We work with commercial customers to organise appropriate inspection, testing and electrical maintenance activities.

Our services can include electrical inspections, testing, maintenance work and follow-up repairs where required.

Scheduled Fire Alarm Servicing

We provide ongoing fire alarm inspection, testing and maintenance for commercial premises.

Our team can assist with scheduled servicing, system faults and maintenance requirements involving existing fire alarm equipment.

More on fire alarm maintenance

Responsive Support When Problems Occur

Electrical faults and system problems can disrupt normal business operations.

Our team provides reactive electrical and fire alarm maintenance services to commercial customers, investigating reported faults and undertaking appropriate repairs.

24/7, 365-Day Support for Registered Business Clients

A.C. Heating Services provides round-the-clock call-out support to registered commercial clients with established eligible service arrangements.

This service is available 24 hours a day, 365 days a year, to help eligible business customers obtain support when urgent issues arise.

Important: This is a registered-client service, not a general public emergency call-out offering.

Existing clients should use their agreed emergency contact arrangements.
