Electrical Inspection, Testing & Certification

Electrical inspection and testing help property owners and managers understand the condition of electrical installations and identify potential safety concerns.

We provide professional inspection and testing services for domestic and commercial customers.

Electrical Installation Condition Reports (EICRs)

An EICR assesses the condition of an existing electrical installation and identifies observations, defects or issues requiring attention.

We undertake EICRs for customers seeking to assess the condition of their electrical systems.

Portable Appliance Testing (PAT)

We provide Portable Appliance Testing for businesses and other customers requiring inspection and testing of electrical equipment.

Electrical Installation Certificates (EICs)

We provide Electrical Installation Certificates for qualifying new installations and electrical alterations, following the appropriate inspection and testing of the work.

Fault Finding & Remedial Work

Where electrical problems or defects are identified, our team can investigate issues and undertake appropriate remedial electrical works.
