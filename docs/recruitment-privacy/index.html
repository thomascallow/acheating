Recruitment Privacy Notice

This notice explains how A.C. Heating Services Ltd uses information you send us when you apply for a role or enquire about an apprenticeship.

Who we are

A.C. Heating Services Ltd, La Maison Blanche, Les Hubits, St Martin, Guernsey GY4 6LU. Applications are received at acheating@cwgsy.net.

Information we collect

Your CV, covering message, contact details, qualifications, work history and any other information you choose to send. If we take an application forward, we may also collect information from interviews and from referees you name.

How we use it

We use your information to assess your application, contact you about it and, if you are offered a role, to prepare for your employment.

Your application is seen only by the directors and the members of staff involved in recruiting for the role. We do not sell your information or share it with third parties, except where this is needed to progress your application: for example, contacting referees you have named, or, for apprenticeship applicants, sharing relevant details with the apprenticeship training provider.

How long we keep it

If your application is unsuccessful, we keep your information for six months after the recruitment process has finished and then securely delete it. If you would like us to keep your details on file for future vacancies, let us know and we will hold them for up to twelve months. You can ask us to delete them at any time before then.

If you are offered a role and accept it, your application will form part of your employment record.

Your rights

Under the Data Protection (Bailiwick of Guernsey) Law, 2017, you have the right to ask to see the information we hold about you, to have it corrected or deleted, to object to or restrict how we use it, and to withdraw your application at any time. To make a request, email acheating@cwgsy.net.

If you are unhappy with how we have handled your information, you can complain to the Office of the Data Protection Authority in Guernsey (www.odpa.gg). We would appreciate the chance to resolve your concern first, so please contact us in the first instance.

Changes to this notice

We may update this notice from time to time. The latest version will always be published on this page.
