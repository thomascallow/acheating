Privacy Policy

This notice explains how A.C. Heating Services Ltd (“we”, “us”) uses personal information when you contact us or use this website.

Who we are

A.C. Heating Services Ltd, La Maison Blanche, Les Hubits, St Martin, Guernsey GY4 6LU. Telephone 07781 100610. Email acheating@cwgsy.net.

We are the controller of the personal information described in this notice and are responsible for looking after it in line with the Data Protection (Bailiwick of Guernsey) Law, 2017.

Information we collect

When you send an enquiry, email or call us, we may receive your name, company name, email address, telephone number, the address or location of a property, details of the work you need and any photographs or documents you choose to send.

How we use it

We use this information to respond to your enquiry, prepare quotations, arrange and carry out work you request, manage any service arrangement with your business and keep appropriate business records.

We do not sell your information. We share it only where this is needed to run our business or deliver the work you have asked for, for example with our accountants and professional advisers, with the providers of our IT and email services, and, where a fire alarm system is connected to monitoring, with the Alarm Receiving Centre. We may also disclose information where we are required to do so by law.

How long we keep it

If an enquiry or quotation does not lead to any work, we keep your details for up to twelve months and then delete them.

For customers, we keep job and account records for at least six years after the work is completed, to meet our tax and accounting obligations. Certificates and records relating to electrical installations and fire alarm systems may be kept for longer, because they relate to the ongoing safety of the installation and may be needed for future inspection, testing or maintenance.

Your rights

Under the Data Protection (Bailiwick of Guernsey) Law, 2017, you have the right to ask to see the personal information we hold about you, to have it corrected or deleted, and to object to or restrict how we use it. To make a request, contact us using the details above.

If you are unhappy with how we have handled your information, you can complain to the Office of the Data Protection Authority in Guernsey (www.odpa.gg). We would appreciate the chance to resolve your concern first, so please contact us in the first instance.

Cookies and website services

This website does not set cookies and does not use analytics or advertising tracking.

Fonts on this website are loaded from Google Fonts, which means your browser connects to Google’s servers when you visit.

If the website later adds analytics or other tools that use cookies, this section will be updated before they are switched on.

Recruitment

If you apply for a job or apprenticeship, see our Recruitment Privacy Notice.

Changes to this notice

We may update this notice from time to time. The latest version will always be published on this page.
