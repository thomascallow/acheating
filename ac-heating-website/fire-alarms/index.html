Specialist Fire Alarm Services for Guernsey Businesses

Reliable fire detection is an important part of protecting commercial premises, their occupants and business operations.

A.C. Heating Services provides specialist fire alarm installation, commissioning, testing and maintenance services for commercial customers across Guernsey.

Our team works with Kentec fire alarm equipment and supports customers with new installations, existing systems, ongoing maintenance and Alarm Receiving Centre connections.

Professional Fire Alarm Installations

We undertake fire alarm installation work for commercial premises, including new systems and alterations to existing installations.

Our team can assist with fire alarm requirements arising from new installations, refurbishments and changes to existing buildings.

We work with customers to understand their requirements and establish the scope of installation work.

Experience with Kentec Fire Alarm Systems

Our team works with Kentec fire alarm equipment, providing installation, commissioning, testing and ongoing servicing.

We also undertake modifications and maintenance of existing systems where appropriate.

Fire Alarm Commissioning & Testing

Commissioning and testing are important parts of establishing and maintaining the operation of fire alarm systems.

We provide commissioning and testing services for qualifying fire alarm installations and undertake testing as part of ongoing servicing and maintenance.

Planned Fire Alarm Maintenance

Fire alarm systems require appropriate inspection, testing and maintenance to support their continued operation.

We provide scheduled fire alarm servicing and maintenance for commercial customers throughout Guernsey.

Our services include inspection, testing, investigation of faults and maintenance of existing systems.

For businesses requiring regular servicing, we can discuss suitable planned maintenance arrangements.

Ask About Fire Alarm Maintenance

Alarm Receiving Centre (ARC) Connections

We provide fire alarm connections to Alarm Receiving Centres.

These arrangements can support remote alarm signal monitoring by an appropriate receiving centre, subject to the agreed connection and monitoring service.

Contact our team to discuss ARC connection requirements for your premises.

Long-Term Fire Alarm Support

Our relationship with a customer does not have to end once a new system has been installed.

We work with commercial clients who require ongoing servicing, maintenance and responsive support for their fire alarm systems.

Registered commercial clients with eligible service arrangements can benefit from access to our 24-hour, 365-day call-out service.
