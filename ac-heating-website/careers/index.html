Careers at A.C. Heating Services

Build Your Career with a Trusted Guernsey Electrical Contractor

Join a local team. Work on varied projects. Help support Guernsey’s businesses.

See who we’re looking for How to apply

A.C. Heating Services Ltd is a family-owned electrical contracting business established in 1984 and based in St Martin, Guernsey.

As demand for our electrical and fire alarm services continues to grow, we’re looking for motivated people interested in joining our team.

Our work covers a wide variety of commercial, industrial and domestic electrical projects, from new installations and refurbishments to specialist fire alarm systems, inspection, testing and maintenance.

Whether you’re an experienced electrician or looking to develop your career through an apprenticeship, we’d be pleased to hear from you.

Who We’re Looking For

Qualified Electricians

We’re interested in hearing from qualified electricians who would like to join our growing business.

Experience in any of the following areas would be particularly relevant:

  • Commercial electrical installations
  • Industrial electrical work
  • Electrical fault finding
  • Inspection and testing
  • Electrical maintenance
  • Fire alarm installation or maintenance

We value professional workmanship, reliability, initiative and a positive approach to working with colleagues and customers.
